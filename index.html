Publications

CVPR 2026
FastGaMer: Efficient GainMap Learning for Practical Inverse Tone Mapping
Y. Guan, R. Xu, C. Chen, Y. Liao, D. Song, F. Song, Z. Xiong

We introduce FastGaMer, a structured inverse tone mapping framework that reconstructs per-channel color gain maps to jointly restore compressed dynamic range and wide color gamut from locally tone-mapped SDR inputs. Local and global degradations are inverted separately via dynamic bilateral grids and learnable 3D LUTs, with all high-resolution operations being network-free. FastGaMer outperforms prior lightweight ITM methods by +1.4 dB PQ-PSNR while reducing runtime by 70%, processing 4K images in only 6.2 ms.

CVPR 2026
RawMetaDiff: Unlocking Extreme Darkness from Dual-Exposure RAW with Meta-Guided Diffusion
P. Liu, J. Xia*, Y. Guan, Y. Li, Z. Lang, R. Xu, C. Chen, D. Song, F. Song, Z. Xiong

We propose RawMetaDiff, a generative diffusion framework for extreme low-light Raw image restoration from dual-exposure inputs guided by Raw metadata. Two complementary mechanisms—Meta-Assistant Color Transfer for global color consistency and Meta-Normed Cross Attention for robust cross-exposure correspondence—enable high-fidelity detail recovery and color preservation. Extensive experiments on both synthetic and a newly collected 1K real-world dual-exposure dataset demonstrate significant improvements over existing methods.

ECCV 2026
Reflect-R1: Evidence-Driven Reflection for Self-Correction in Long Video Understanding
S. Chen, Y. Chen*, Y. Guan*, Z. Cheng, Z. Zhang, S. Qin, B. Xia, J. Li, W. Yang, F. Ma

We propose Reflect-R1, the first evidence-driven self-correction framework for long video understanding that breaks the hallucination loop through dynamic visual evidence retrieval across a three-stage pipeline of intuition, verification, and arbitration. A stage-decoupled reinforcement learning algorithm (SD-GRPO) independently computes advantage functions across reasoning stages to resolve policy coupling. Experiments on VideoMME and LongVideoBench demonstrate state-of-the-art performance with significantly improved genuine rectification rates.

HDR Image Generation via Gain Map Decomposed Diffusion
Y. Guan, R. Xu, Y. Liao, M. Yao, L. Wang, Z. Xiong

We introduce a gain-map decomposed diffusion pipeline that delivers the first text-to-HDR image generator, stays compatible with ControlNet and mainstream Stable Diffusion extensions, and scales SDR-to-HDR synthesis to native 4K resolution.

ICLR 2026
Arbitrary-Shaped Image Generation via Spherical Neural Field Diffusion
J. Xia, Y. Guan, R. Xu, Z. Xiong

We perform diffusion over spherical neural fields with latent sampling, enabling explicit control over viewpoint, field of view, and output resolution to generate perspective, panoramic, and fisheye imagery with sharply improved texture detail and geometric consistency.

We investigate diffusion priors for HDR video reconstruction, treating noisy or motion-blurred frames as out-of-distribution samples for targeted refinement, and achieve up to 15% LPIPS improvements with cleaner temporal consistency.
